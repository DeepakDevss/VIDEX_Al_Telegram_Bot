# Privacy Policy — VIDEX (Telegram bot @Linnet_Agents_Bot)

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

This policy explains, in plain language, what VIDEX ("the bot", "we", "us") does with
information when you message it. It is written to match what the bot actually does — nothing
here is aspirational.

## 1. Who we are

VIDEX is a personal/community AI assistant that runs as a Telegram bot at **@Linnet_Agents_Bot**.
The developer is Deepak Danodia (https://github.com/DeepakDevss). Contact:
**coresovereign.official@gmail.com**.

VIDEX is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Telegram.

## 2. You are talking to an AI

Replies are generated by a large language model. They can be wrong. VIDEX is **not** a
substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or safety advice — for anything
important, check with a qualified human.

## 3. What we store

Stored on the server that runs the bot, **separately for each user (keyed by your Telegram
user ID)**:

- Your Telegram user ID, first name and username
- Your interface language, and the chats you appear in
- Counters: how many messages you have sent, when you were first and last seen
- **Notes and a short profile** the bot keeps so it can remember you — these come from things
  you tell it, and you can see and delete them at any time
- **Reminders** you ask for (the text and the time they are due)

Kept only in memory (RAM) while the bot is running, and **not** written to storage:

- A short recent transcript of the chat (used to follow the conversation), which is dropped
  after the chat has been idle for a while
- Media you send (photos, voice notes, files) is processed to answer you and is not kept by
  the bot afterwards

We do **not** ask for your phone number, email, contacts or precise location, and we do not
store them.

## 4. Why we use it

- To reply to you and remember context, so the bot does not repeat itself
- To deliver the reminders you set
- To prevent abuse and keep the service working
- To keep basic, non-identifying usage counts (for example, how many users the bot has)

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your conversations to train AI models.

## 5. Who else sees it (sub-processors)

To generate a reply, the text of your message — and any image, voice note or document you send
— is sent to an AI provider:

- **Google (Gemini API)** — main model, image understanding, text-to-speech. See Google's
  privacy policy and the Gemini API terms.
- **Groq** — fallback chat model and speech-to-text (Whisper) when the main one is unavailable.
  See Groq's privacy policy.
- **Pollinations** — keyless text fallback and image generation, when enabled. See
  Pollinations' terms.

Web lookups (for live facts) go to public sources such as DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia and Google
News; the search text is what you asked about.

The bot is hosted on a third-party platform (for example Render). Hosting and its logs are
governed by that provider's policy.

Your message content may be processed in countries other than your own (including the United
States, where these providers operate).

## 6. Legal basis (GDPR, where it applies)

- **Consent** — you message the bot by your own choice.
- **Legitimate interests** — answering you, keeping the service secure, and preventing abuse.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Your memory and profile** — until you delete it with `/forget`, or until it is removed by
  the automatic retention sweep (currently after about 90 days with no activity).
- **Reminders** — until they fire, or you delete them, or the retention sweep removes them.
- **Recent chat transcript** — only in memory, and dropped once the chat is idle.
- **Server logs** — kept by the hosting provider for a short period for debugging.

On free hosting the storage is **ephemeral**: memory can be lost when the service restarts or
redeploys.

## 8. Your controls

- `/memory` — see what the bot has stored about you
- `/forget` — delete everything the bot stored about you
- `/reminders` and `/delremind` — manage your reminders
- Email **coresovereign.official@gmail.com** to ask for access, correction or deletion
- You may complain to your local data-protection authority

## 9. Children

The bot is not intended for anyone under 13, or under 16 if you are in the European Union. We
do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the bot, contact
us and we will delete the data.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable measures — data is kept per user, memory writes are sanitised, and message
content is treated as untrusted to resist prompt-injection. No system is perfectly secure, so
please do not send anything you would not want processed by an AI provider.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will change, and continued use after
a change means you accept the new version. The current version always lives in the project
repository (`privacy-policy.md`) and is available in the bot with `/privacy`.
